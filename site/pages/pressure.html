Where change and opportunity meet

The regulation layer shows which work faces legal change, and when. The AI layer shows where AI could change the work. Put them together and you can see where both pressures land on the same part of the organisation. That's where redesign, investment and governance need the most attention.

Useful for:Business strategyExecutive teams and boardsTransformation portfoliosCompliance leads
  1. Each bubble is a function area, sized by its number of activities.
  2. Further up means more of its work faces regulatory change. Further right means more of it could change with AI.
  3. Select a bubble to see what's changing, when, and where AI could help.
