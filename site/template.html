What a housing association actually does

A shared map of the work of an English housing association, from answering a repair call to submitting the regulatory returns. It's organised into service areas, services and activities so that people can talk about the same work in the same terms, whether they're redesigning a service, choosing a system or planning a change.

About this model

The structure is based on what every English provider has to do: the Regulator of Social Housing's consumer and economic standards, landlord safety duties (gas, electrical, fire, asbestos, water and lifts), the Building Safety Act, HHSRS, Awaab's Law, the Housing Ombudsman's Complaint Handling Code and the Tenant Satisfaction Measures, plus the way the sector usually organises its teams and costs.

I was originally inspired to create this after reading Rannoch Associates' paper "The impact of AI on Housing Associations" (October 2026). That model wasn't published, but it did make me think that a common and open model for services could be useful for others in the sector, so this is an independent attempt, not their work. I was also inspired by Dave Skinners work around Housing Service Patterns. Every name and grouping is a judgement about a generic association. Real organisations differ, and that's what makes it worth improving together.
