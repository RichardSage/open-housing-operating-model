# Code of conduct

This project brings together people from across social housing, with different roles, organisations and views. We want it to be a welcoming, useful place for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming you've understood.
- Share knowledge generously, and credit others' work.
- Keep residents' and colleagues' personal information out of the project. Never post details that could identify a tenant, a case or a member of staff.
- Don't use the project to promote products or services.

## What isn't acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove content, or block people who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
